Spain leads on both volume (66,578 sessions) and revenue (€108,363); the US, UK, France and Germany convert efficiently behind it. The opposite pattern dominates the session count: Türkiye (58,061) and the Gulf — Kuwait, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, UAE — draw 175,760 sessions and produce essentially no bookings. Excluding or capping these geos (or moving PMax onto Google Ads conversion data now that attribution has improved) is the clearest efficiency lever.
