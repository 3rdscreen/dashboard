Spain leads on both volume (61,024 sessions) and revenue (€101,741); the US, UK, France and Germany convert efficiently behind it. The opposite pattern dominates the session count: Türkiye (58,061) and the Gulf — Kuwait, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, UAE — draw 175,606 sessions and produce essentially no bookings.
