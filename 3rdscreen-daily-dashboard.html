How to read this. Hero numbers are yesterday vs last 7 days vs month-to-date — today's decision in the context of the trend. Ecommerce leads on ROAS and website purchases; lead accounts on cost per lead and website leads. The arrow compares yesterday to the 7-day average.

Conversion rate bar shows this month's rate (results ÷ landing-page views) with the healthy average for that business marked as the dashed green line — so you see at a glance whether the account is above or below where it should be. Bands are directional industry estimates, not Meta-verified.

Scale-ready means the account's last-7-day ROAS is above 3.5 — a strong single day isn't enough. Individual ad sets above target can still be scaled inside an account that isn't.

Full-funnel (inside each ecommerce client) traces landing-page views → adds to cart → checkouts initiated → purchases, and flags the exact step where people drop off — so a weak account can be diagnosed as a product-page, cart, checkout, or purchase-completion issue rather than guessed at.
